HOOK FORMULA
Opening: Show an object; demonstrate before context.
Promise: How does it work, and how can it be broken?
Payoff: Solve the small puzzle by ~2.5% runtime; resolve the larger question, then open a harder mystery.
Reference openers: “This is the Enigma”; “You'd never guess what it is.”

SPOKEN VOICE
Average: 14.9 words/sentence; ≥7 fragments : 555 full (~1:79; minimum).
Markers/min: so 2.55; now 0.65; just 0.51; actually 0.40; okay/like 0.38 each; right 0.27.
Intensifiers/adverbs: 0.95/min. Restarts: 11 (0.23/min); don't manufacture. Contractions: mostly.
I/we/you: 36% / 22% / 42%.
Spoken references: “open this up” — narrates action; “I'm struggling a bit” — admits difficulty; “I googled it” — admits lookup; “Pencils down, everyone” — marks result; “Bingo” — instant reaction.

WORD-CHOICE HABITS
Says “fanatics,” not “enthusiasts,” because it signals intensity.
Says “naked,” not “unencrypted,” because it creates an image.
Says “cillies,” not “predictable settings,” because labels stick.
Says “crib,” not “clue,” because it names a method.
Says “Bingo,” not “success,” because it sounds spontaneous.

PERSONALITY MOVES
Prior belief/surprise: 1 prediction visibly overturned in a demonstration.
Not knowing: 2 “I don't know” uses; 1 explicit lookup admission.
Difficulty: 1 sustained live attempt with failed guesses.

HUMOR AND FACT DELIVERY
Humor: 11 moments (~0.23/min); contrast, wordplay, self-deprecation, understatement.
Facts: demonstrate, then explain with examples; escalate numbers by showing how components multiply. Use dates for chronology, comparisons for scale. Reference: “36 Enigma machines working in parallel.”

STRUCTURE MAP
Hook/demo 5% > foundations 17% > history/breakthrough 12% > live attempt 12% > sponsor/Turing setup 8% > logic/Bombe 28% > consequences 12% > final mystery 3% > CTA 1% (approx.).
Re-hooks: ~15 (0.32/min); add an obstacle, prediction, “what if,” or reversal after a payoff.

ENDING AND CTA
Ends on an unresolved challenge. CTA at ~99%: decrypt its message and report back; casual, direct.
Last sentence: open invitation — “Let us know if you can.”

RULES FOR THE SCRIPT AI
1. Open on a specific object or observable event; skip greetings. (high)
2. Demonstrate the opening mechanism within 6% of runtime. (high)
3. Give each technical principle one concrete example before defining it. (high)
4. Re-hook every 3–4 minutes with a new obstacle or question. (medium)
5. Include one genuine prediction or test the viewer can follow. (high)
6. Admit uncertainty only when a real knowledge gap or attempt supports it. (high)
7. Escalate at least 3 times from a solved problem to a harder constraint. (high)
8. End with one specific challenge or next question, not a generic CTA. (high)

DO NOT COPY
Do not assume access to authentic hardware, museum locations or specialist interviewees.
Do not imitate the live decryption attempt without a credible, demonstrable task.
