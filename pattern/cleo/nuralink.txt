HOOK FORMULA
- Starts with surprising action; no greeting or definition.
- Promise: what is its significance beyond fantasy?
- Payoff: answer practical questions mid-video; resolve deeper promise in final 15%, returning to initial belief.
- “This robot is putting a computer chip into a human brain.”
- “This story isn't about trying to make us more like machines.”

SPOKEN VOICE
- Average ~13.2 words/sentence; fragments ~1:15 full sentences (estimate).
- Markers/min: so 1.53; you know 0.17; I think 0.23; kind of 0.17; right? 0.06.
- Intensifiers/adverbs/min: really 0.85; actually 0.74; just 0.45; much 0.40; very 0.28.
- Repetition: ~5 repeated-word pairs/17.7 min; emphasis plus speech artifacts.
- Contractions: mostly. I/we/you ratio: 61:44:132.
- “why is everything a fruit?” — comic self-interruption.
- “I don't like it.” — blunt visceral reaction.
- “That's way more recent than I thought.” — admits changed expectation.
- “What am I looking at here?” — live curious reaction.
- “Not today.” — dry comic boundary.

WORD-CHOICE HABITS
- says “squishy,” not “soft,” because tactile and immediate.
- says “Whoa,” not “interesting,” because spontaneous reaction.
- says “actually,” not “in fact,” because casual correction.
- says “nosebleeds,” not “distant seats,” because spatial shorthand.
- says “microphones,” not “sensors,” because familiar function.

PERSONALITY MOVES
- Prior belief: stated/revisited twice (3:58, 15:35).
- Wrong/surprised: 2 corrections (5:19, 15:35).
- Uncertainty: 4+ admissions (4:02, 14:22, 14:29, 17:05).

HUMOR AND FACT DELIVERY
- ~13 light beats/17.7 min (~0.7/min): reactions, absurd comparisons, asides.
- Facts/numbers: mostly analogies; dates/labels plain. Reference: “as heavy as a supermarket chicken.”

STRUCTURE MAP
- Hook 6% > sensory setup 17% > misconception 8% > comparison ladder 21% > sponsor 6% > build/demo 13% > future/limits 14% > applications/payoff/CTA 15%.
- Re-hooks: ~15/17.7 min (~70 sec apart): questions, contrasts, delayed reveals, “but” pivots.

ENDING AND CTA
- Thesis callback, then subscription ask; no cliffhanger.
- CTA at 17:40/end: subscribe for more science/tech; soft, casual, benefit-led.
- Last sentence: brief ask. Reference: “support our show by subscribing.”

RULES FOR THE SCRIPT AI
1. Open with a concrete action or surprising claim in sentence one. (high)
2. State the central question within 30 seconds. (high)
3. Revisit the narrator's starting belief in the final 15%. (high)
4. Give each major technical idea one familiar analogy. (high)
5. Use at least 3 genuine reaction lines per 10 minutes. (medium)
6. Start each major section with a question, contrast, problem, or reveal. (high)
7. Separate demonstrated facts from speculation; mark uncertainty. (high)
8. Place a one-sentence thesis callback before the CTA. (high)
